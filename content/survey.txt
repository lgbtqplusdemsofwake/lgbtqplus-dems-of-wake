Survey

Hi,
I'm Katie Day.  I am an at large board member of the LGBTQ+ Dems of Wake County.
This is a survey for candidates in the 2026 North Carolina Primaries.  This survey is antagonistic by design.  It will make you uncomfortable.  Discomfort is required to help those in greater discomfort.

I hope this survey receives the blessing of the LGBTQ+ Dems of Wake County, Mecklenberg County, and NC.

I know your campaign manager is overloaded with 40 similar surveys.  This survey is designed to take minimal time by being yes/no/let me ask a friend/I don't know and I don't have a friend that I can ask/this is a loaded question.

A score card will be created for each candidate that receives this survey.  Answering the survey guarantees a higher score.
Some answers will be fact checked and that will be considered against the score.
Some score cards will be annotated with "This candidate is bad.  We will survive slightly longer if this candidate is elected."
The score cards will be published and distributed to political and activist groups.


Questions:
1. Candidate Consent

    1. Do you consent to the publication of my long form answers. (a=2, c=1, d=1)

    a. yes; b. no; c. I need to speak with my counsel; d. I don't know; e. this is a loaded question (mu)

2. Suitability

    1. Do you believe bargaining with a tyrant works? (b=2)

    2. Do you believe being elected/remaining in office is more important than your most marginalized constituents? (b=2)

    3. Do you believe believe you should accept money from interests that displace or delete marginalized populations? (b=2) (fact check)

    4. Do you believe believe it's acceptable to further interests that displace or delete marginalized populations after you accept the money from those interests? (b=2) (fact check)

    6. Do you believe you should hide threats from interests that displace or delete marginalized populations when you decline money or agenda from those interests? (b=2, c=1, d=1) (fact check)

    7. Describe your suitability for office in terms a 6th grader can understand (optional).
